THE LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER
A Play in One Act
by Jane Doe

CHARACTERS
MARGARET, a lighthouse keeper, 60s
TOM, her nephew, 20s

SCENE 1

A lighthouse at night. Wind.

MARGARET
You're late.

TOM
(shaking off rain)
The ferry was cancelled. I walked from the point.

MARGARET
In this?

(She hands Tom a towel.)

TOM
Thanks. Is there tea?

MARGARET
There's always tea.

Lights fade.
